The Reality

Two students graduate. Both pass their exams.

Student A

Knows how to think critically, manage time, communicate ideas, solve problems, work with others, and confidently use AI.

Student B

Does not. Same grades, same certificate, a very different decade ahead.

Ten years later, the difference between them won't be intelligence. It will be the habits and skills they built long before the workforce, the very thing school rarely teaches.

Mikaelson School Club04
The Problem

School teaches what to learn, not how to grow.

Habits aren't taught

Young people are told what to achieve but rarely taught how to build the consistency to get there. Discipline is a skill, it must be practised deliberately.

The window is narrow

Ages 10–16 is when identity, habits, and character are actively forming. A weekly programme in this window creates durable change no single event can replicate.

The economy is moving faster

AI, digital marketing, and software thinking are reshaping every Nigerian industry. Students will enter a workforce that expects these as baseline, not specialist, skills.

Mikaelson School Club05
The Opportunity

Nigeria has one of the world's largest youth populations, and AI is reshaping every path into work.

Employers increasingly value adaptability, communication, creativity, leadership, and digital literacy alongside grades. Schools can't solve this alone, they need practical programmes that fit existing structures without disrupting learning.

Mikaelson School Club10
The Programme · The Core

Habit development & personal formation.

One term, 12 weekly sessions, led by a school-based Facilitator and Student Leads drawn from the student body. Everything else, including digital skills, exists in service of this.

From Session 1, each student tracks specific habits, reflects in their journal, and makes one small commitment for the week ahead. Woven throughout: self-awareness, consistency, accountability, time, confidence, and learning how to learn, the infrastructure of a capable, self-directed human being.

Session Structure · Every Week

Predictability is the point.

SegmentWhat happensTime
Check-InEach student shares one win and one challenge. Every voice is heard, no one is skipped. Builds honesty, community, and self-reflection.10 min
Core SessionGuided discussion or activity on the week's theme. In Sessions 4–10, includes the digital-skills component.25–30 min
ReflectionStudents journal and connect the lesson to their own lives; habit tracker updated. The internal work happens here.15 min
Weekly CommitmentEach student names one specific thing to practise before next session. Written down, revisited the following week.5–10 min

The structure is the same every week, because consistency is what the programme is teaching.
